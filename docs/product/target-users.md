# Target Users

## Primary users

### Small ecommerce operator

Runs a small store and prepares images personally. Needs fast results, sensible defaults, and no design-software learning curve.

Typical tasks:

- Reduce upload size
- Convert an iPhone image
- Crop for a storefront
- Produce consistent dimensions
- Prepare a batch of product images

### Marketplace seller

Sells through platforms with image constraints. Needs repeatable exports and marketplace-specific presets.

Typical tasks:

- Meet file-size limits
- Use square or fixed-ratio crops
- Remove incompatible formats
- Prepare multiple listings consistently

### Ecommerce developer or agency

Builds and maintains stores for clients. Needs predictable output and may later benefit from reusable project settings or APIs.

Typical tasks:

- Prepare assets during implementation
- Fix client-provided images
- Standardize catalogs
- Apply presets across projects

## Secondary users

- Graphic designers handling repetitive exports
- Content managers
- Social-commerce sellers
- Product photographers
- Developers needing a quick browser utility

## Not the initial target

- Professional photo retouching workflows
- RAW photo processing
- Full design-canvas replacement
- Digital asset management
- Enterprise approval workflows
